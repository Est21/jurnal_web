Privacy policy · Windows and Android

Your data stays with you.

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Jurnal does not collect, send or share your data. Everything stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. Jurnal has no accounts, cloud sync, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs, and the app makes no network requests. The Android version does not even request the internet permission. This applies to everyone, including children.

Where your data is stored

Windows: Everything you write is stored in a single local vault file (.jurnal) at a location you choose on your own computer. App preferences are also stored locally. Music files you add are stored inside your vault file; the app never sends them anywhere.

Android: Everything you write is stored on your phone, in the app's private storage that only Jurnal can access. App preferences are also stored locally. Automatic backups are kept in that same private storage on your device; Android's cloud / device-to-device app backup is turned off for this app. Data leaves your phone only if you yourself export it with "Share".

You can copy or back up the vault file yourself. If you copy, move or share it (for example to a cloud folder or to another person), that copy is outside the app and outside the developer's control, and anyone who has the file can read its unlocked content. Sections and documents you encrypt cannot be opened without the recovery code or password; if the recovery code is lost, the content cannot be recovered.

Android permissions

The Android version requests only the following; none is used to collect data:

  • Notifications (Android 13+): to show the timer's and ambient-sound controls' notification. The permission is requested only when you first use the timer or ambient sounds.
  • Exact alarms: to deliver the notification at the exact moment the timer ends.

The app does not request internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts or general storage permissions. Images, PDFs and vault files are brought in through Android's system file picker, only the files you choose.

Things that open outside the app

  • Report a bug opens your own email app with a message to jurnalapp@proton.me that includes only the app version and platform. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.
  • Support / Buy Me a Coffee opens an external website in your browser, which has its own privacy policy.
  • Share / Export (Android) hands a backup, vault or exported file to Android's system share sheet; you decide which app receives it. A vault file you open with Jurnal from another app is imported only with your confirmation.

Children's privacy

Because Jurnal collects no personal data at all, it collects none from children either.

Deleting your data

On Windows, delete the vault file and any copies you made. On Android, uninstalling the app deletes the vault and automatic backups in its private storage; copies you exported with "Share" must be deleted separately. There is no server-side record about you to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page and the date above will be updated.
