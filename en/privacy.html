Privacy policy

Your data stays with you.

This text is identical to the Jurnal privacy policy on the Microsoft Store.

Jurnal does not collect, send or share your data. Everything stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. Jurnal has no accounts, cloud sync, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs, and the app makes no network requests. This applies to everyone, including children.

Where your data is stored

Everything you write is stored in a single local vault file (.jurnal) at a location you choose on your own computer. App preferences are also stored locally. Music files you add are stored inside your vault file; the app never sends them anywhere.

You can copy or back up the vault file yourself. If you copy, move or share it (for example to a cloud folder or to another person), that copy is outside the app and outside the developer's control, and anyone who has the file can read its unlocked content. To delete all your data, delete the vault file and any copies you made.

Things that open outside the app

  • Report a bug opens your own email app with a message to jurnalapp@proton.me that includes only the app version and platform. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.
  • Support / Buy Me a Coffee opens an external website in your browser, which has its own privacy policy.

Contact

Questions: jurnalapp@proton.me
