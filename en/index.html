01Why Jurnal

Does little; does it well.

Focus

The interface steps back while you write. Focus and Zen modes are built so a letter appears the instant you press a key.

Privacy

Encryption per section and per document. No master password; a single recovery code that only you hold.

Offline

The app makes no network requests. Your data lives in a single local .jurnal file.

02Features

Everything a writing desk should have.

Journal, documents and board together

Keep a daily journal, write long documents, or think on a free-form board with pen, highlighter, shapes and text. Bring a PDF onto the board and draw over it.

Encryption

A separate key for the journal section and for each document. One shared recovery code.

Timer and sounds

A work timer and ambient sounds that don't pull your attention.

Calendar and library

Find entries by day or in the library; search inside a document.

Export

Export to Markdown, Word, PDF and images; import and back up.

Trash

What you delete stays recoverable for 30 days.

Turkish and English

Two interface languages, light and dark themes, offline spell check.

05FAQ

Frequently asked

Is Jurnal free?

Yes. If you like it, you can support the developer through Buy Me a Coffee; that is entirely optional.

Where is my data stored?

In a single .jurnal file at a location you choose on your computer. The app sends nothing to any server. To back up, simply copy the file.

What if I lose my recovery code?

Encrypted content cannot be recovered without it; this is by design. Save the code somewhere safe when you first lock something.

Which platforms does it run on?

Windows (Microsoft Store) and Android. The Android version is being prepared for Google Play; it will be announced on this page.

I found a bug or have a suggestion.

Use "Report a bug" in the app's Settings, or write to jurnalapp@proton.me.
